Produce a clean high-resolution PNG-style raster asset with true transparent background. No text, no Chinese characters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no frame, no drop shadow, no outer glow, no background scenery, no opaque halo, no full-bleed fill.

Avoid: photorealism, painterly brushwork, comic-book outlines, caricature, dense black areas, halftone dots, scanning noise, paper texture, gradients, cross-hatching everywhere, decorative background elements, centered floating subject, excessive empty margin below the figure, and any invented poster copy.
